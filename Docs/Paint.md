## When and what NOT to paint

Paint **before assembly** — flat pieces on a workbench are much easier
than reaching into a built cabinet. Mask these surfaces with painter's
tape (they must stay raw):

- Edges that will be glued — Confirmat screws need to bite raw wood
- Confirmat hole openings — paint blocks the head from sitting flush

After painting and curing, assemble per `Assembly.md`.

## Recommended workflow: gesso primer + Molotow spray

**Belton Molotow Premium** spray paint has the best matte palette and
goes on flat panels with zero brush marks. Cheap gesso (brushed on)
makes a perfect primer.

**Per panel (~Q150-200 total):**
- Artist's gesso, thinned 10-15% with water (~120 mL is plenty)
- 4" foam mini-roller + small paint tray (for the panel faces)
- Foam brush (for the edges only)
- 1 can Belton Molotow Premium in your color (matte, 400 mL)
- Sandpaper: 120-grit, 180-grit, 220-grit
- N95 mask, large piece of cardboard, drop cloth

### Steps

1. **Sand.** 120 → 180 → 220-grit, with the grain. Wipe dust with a
   damp microfiber.
2. **Prime.** Roll thinned gesso on the faces with the foam roller;
   touch up the edges with a foam brush. 1 coat. Dry ~1h. Light sand
   with 220-grit — more thoroughly if the gesso is semi-gloss (kill
   the sheen so the spray grips). Wipe.
3. **Spray.** Outdoors or in a well-ventilated space. Shake can 2 min.
   Hold 20-30 cm from the surface. Smooth, sweeping passes; start and
   end the spray *past* the edge to avoid edge buildup. 2-3 thin coats,
   15-20 min between. Light sand with 320-grit between coats once dry.
4. **Cure.** 24h before handling roughly. 48-72h before assembly.

## Alternatives

- **Brush + craft acrylic + matte varnish.** For one panel: 60 mL
  acrylic bottle + small matte varnish topcoat from a craft store
  (Kemik, Manualidades y Más). Any color, no spray gear.
- **Latex + brush.** 1/4 gal of Sherwin-Williams **Excello Látex
  Lavable** in mate. Overkill for one panel; right for the whole
  caddy. Needs a wood primer first.

## Common mistakes

- **Painting glue areas.** Confirmat joints fail. Tape them off first.
- **Thick coats.** Drips, sags, never fully cures. Thinner is better.
- **Spraying onto unsanded semi-gloss gesso.** Topcoat peels later.
  Always scuff the primer.
- **Assembling too soon.** Paint feels dry in hours but cures in days.
  Wait 24h+.
